# Water Flow — soul

*Draft for Gal to approve.*

## What this toy is

A sandy board, a spring, and some thirsty plants. You dig ditches so the spring
water reaches every plant, and each one turns green and blooms once it has had
enough to drink. That is the whole idea. What makes it fun is that the water
behaves like water: it runs downhill, fills a low pond before it spills over,
creeps along a ditch at its own pace, and gets swallowed by any hole it
touches. You don't draw a path and win. You dig, then watch the water find its
way, and fix your ditches when it doesn't.

## What it is not

- **Not a race.** No clock counting down, no score for speed. The water takes
  the time it takes, and you can make it go faster if you want.
- **Not a game you lose.** No lives, no game over. A plant that stays dry just
  means you dig again, undo, or start over.
- **Not connect-the-pipes.** Water never jumps along a path the moment it
  links up. It has to actually run there, fill up and spill over.
- **Not a farm or a shop.** No coins, no seeds to buy, no harvest to sell. A
  plant blooms and that's its reward.
- **Not luck.** No random boards and no surprises from the game. The same
  digging always gives the same water.
- **Not a busy world.** No creatures running around, no story scenes. Just the
  board, the water and your shovel.
